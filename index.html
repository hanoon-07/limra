The stones

We began as gem dealers before we were jewellers, and we still source every stone directly. Each one comes with its certificate and, if you ask, the story of where it was cut.

  • Sapphire
    Ceylon and Madagascar, blue through to padparadscha
  • Emerald
    Colombian and Zambian, minor oil only
  • Ruby
    Burmese and Mozambique, calibré and cabochon
  • Tanzanite
    Deep violet-blue, oval and pear
  • Opal
    Ethiopian crystal opal with full play of colour
  • Aquamarine
    Santa Maria blue, pear and cushion
  • Turquoise
    Sleeping Beauty, untreated
  • Onyx
    Polished drops for Deco contrast

The atelier

Limra Gems opened its doors more than twenty years ago as a dealer in loose stones. The workshop came later, when we found that the settings we wanted for our stones did not exist, so we learned to make them.

Bespoke

Bring a stone, a sketch, or a grandmother's ring. We draw, cast and set in-house, with a wax model to approve before any gold is poured. Most commissions take six to eight weeks.

Care and repair

Every Limra piece is cleaned, checked and re-tipped for life, free of charge. We also restore antique Deco jewellery that was not made by us.

Visit the showroom

Walk in any day, or book a private appointment to see stones from the vault. If you are abroad, we ship insured worldwide and arrange video viewings.
